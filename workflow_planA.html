Plan A Case

Expected Course

  • Goal is extubation within 6 hours (often sooner).
  • Plan for ICU stay overnight.
  • Transfer out of ICU on POD#1 if stable.

Reasons to Delay Plan

  • High chest tube output
  • Low cardiac index
  • Escalating inotropes or vasopressors
  • Limited responsiveness
  • Failure to meet extubation criteria

POD#1 Goals

  • Mobilize patient
  • Begin diuresis
  • Remove chest tubes if appropriate
  • Remove unnecessary lines
  • Transfer patient to floor
