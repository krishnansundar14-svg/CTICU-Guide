Receiving Patients from the OR

Before Arrival

  • Start preliminary handoff note
  • Document PMH and surgery type

Handover from OR Team

Surgeons
  • Past history
  • Surgery details
  • Drains and tubes
  • Postoperative plan and concerns
Anesthesiologists
  • Airway
  • Lines
  • Intraoperative course
  • Echo report (note LV and RV function)
  • I/O including blood products
  • Current rhythm, hemodynamics, drips
  • Paralysis reversed

First Few Hours After Arrival

  • Monitor bleeding (>200 ml/hr → inform attending surgeon)
  • Monitor labs, chest X‑ray, vitals, rhythm, hemodynamics, I/O
  • MAP goal 65–85 mm Hg
  • If PA catheter present: CI >2.2 and SvO₂ >60
  • Patients are often cold → start Bair Hugger
