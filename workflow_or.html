Receiving patients from the OR

Before arrival

  • Start prelim handoff note.
  • Note PMH, Surgery type.

Handover from OR team

Surgeons

  • Past history
  • Surgery details
  • Drains, tubes
  • Postoperative plan and concerns

Anesthesiologists

  • Airway
  • Lines
  • Intraoperative course
  • Echo report (note LV and RV function)
  • I/O, including blood products
  • Current rhythm, Hemodynamics, current drips
  • Paralysis reversed?

First few hours after arrival

  • Monitor for bleeding. >200 ml/hr: inform attending surgeon
  • Monitor labs, chest Xray, vitals, rhythm, hemodynamics, I/O
  • Usual MAP goals 65-85 mm Hg
  • If PA catheter present: CI >2.2, SvO2> 60
  • Patients are often cold. Get Bair Hugger started
