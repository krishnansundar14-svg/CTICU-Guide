Receiving Patients from OR

Before Arrival

  • Start preliminary handoff note
  • Document PMH and surgery type

Handoff

  • Surgical details and postoperative plan
  • Airway and lines
  • Echo findings (LV/RV function)
  • I/O including blood products
  • Rhythm, hemodynamics, drips

First Hours

  • Monitor bleeding (>200 ml/hr notify surgeon)
  • Review labs, chest X‑ray, vitals
  • MAP goal 65–85
  • If PA catheter: CI >2.2, SvO2 >60
  • Warm patient
