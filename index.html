CTICU Quick Reference Guide

General Workflow

Receiving Patients From OR

Before arrival
  • Start preliminary handoff note
  • Document PMH and surgery type
Handoff
  • Surgeons: history, surgery details, drains/tubes, postop plan
  • Anesthesia: airway, lines, intraop course, echo LV/RV function, I/O, rhythm, drips, paralysis reversal
First hours
  • Monitor bleeding (>200 ml/hr notify surgeon)
  • Review labs, CXR, hemodynamics
  • MAP goal 65‑85
  • CI >2.2, SvO₂ >60 if PA catheter
  • Warm patient

Plan A Case

  • Extubate within 6 hours
  • Transfer out of ICU next day
Reasons to delay
  • High chest tube output
  • Low cardiac index
  • Escalating pressors
  • Limited responsiveness
  • Unable to meet extubation criteria
POD1
  • Mobilize
  • Diuresis
  • Chest tube removal
  • Line removal
  • Transfer to floor

Sending Patient to OR

  • NPO immediately
  • APP or fellow posts case
  • Call CTOR charge nurse
  • Perfusion pager if MCS or CPB needed
  • Notify cardiac anesthesia
  • Prepare blood products
  • Optimize tubing/drips for transport
  • Consent family

Morning Rounds

  • Present hemodynamics, respiratory data, CXR
  • Discuss major concerns
  • Review chest tube output
  • Discuss tube removal
  • Discuss diuresis

Communication Rules

  • Overcommunication preferred
  • Notify surgeons of major deviations
  • Bleeding >200 ml/hr
  • Unexpected chest tube change
  • Worsening cardiogenic shock
  • Inotrope changes
  • Always coordinate vent changes with RT
  • Always coordinate ECMO changes with ECMO specialist

Cardiac Surgeries

CABG

  • Document bypassed vessels
Patient deteriorating
  • Tamponade
  • Graft occlusion → possible cath lab
POD1
  • Beta blocker
  • Aspirin
  • Statin
  • Lasix

SAVR

  • Note valve type and size
  • Daily anticoagulation discussion
Patient deteriorating
  • Tamponade
  • RCA occlusion
  • Hypovolemia in LVH
  • Afib intolerance

Mitral Valve Repair

  • Note SAM tendency
  • Assess LV function
