CTICU Quick Reference Guide

General Workflow

Receiving Patients From OR

Before arrival
  • Start preliminary handoff note
  • Document PMH and surgery type
Handover from OR team
  • Surgeons
    • Past history
    • Surgery details
    • Drains and tubes
    • Postoperative plan
  • Anesthesiology
    • Airway
    • Lines
    • Intraoperative course
    • Echo (LV and RV function)
    • I/O and blood products
    • Current rhythm and drips
    • Paralysis reversed
First few hours
  • Monitor bleeding (>200 ml/hr notify surgeon)
  • Review labs, CXR, vitals and hemodynamics
  • MAP goal 65‑85
  • CI >2.2 and SvO2 >60 if PA catheter present
  • Warm patient with Bair Hugger

Plan A Post‑Operative Pathway

  • Extubate within 6 hours
  • Transfer out of ICU next day
Reasons to delay
  • High chest tube output
  • Low cardiac index
  • Escalating pressors or inotropes
  • Limited responsiveness
  • Failed extubation criteria
POD1
  • Mobilize
  • Diuresis
  • Chest tube removal
  • Line removal
  • Transfer to floor

Cardiac Surgeries

CABG

  • Document bypassed vessels
If patient deteriorates
  • Tamponade
  • Graft occlusion → consider cath lab
POD1 medications
  • Beta blockers
  • Aspirin
  • Statin
  • Lasix

Surgical Aortic Valve Replacement

  • Document valve type and size
  • Discuss anticoagulation daily
If patient deteriorates
  • Tamponade
  • RCA occlusion
  • Hypovolemia in LVH
  • Pulmonary edema with diastolic dysfunction
  • Atrial fibrillation intolerance
